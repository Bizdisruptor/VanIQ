Critical safety notes

CCA wire prohibited. High vibration and moisture inside vehicle framing cause stress-fracturing and galvanic crystallization in Copper Clad Aluminum. All power and accessory runs must use pure tinned-copper marine-grade cable.

Velit 4000 must never be on a hard switch. Cutting source current while the chamber is fired prevents the automatic cool-down fan cycle, causing meltdown of electrical parts. Operate by thermostat controller only.

Bed and garage zone (GB) is wired as an independent local grid — reading lights, over-tank LEDs, and 12V socket plus USB hub use integrated base switches rather than running loops forward to the main panel. Not included in the manifest above.
