Pressure core layout (PC)

PC is structured into two zones: the rear 55" carries the 33-gallon water tank below the floor, with the Isotemp Slim 25L water heater on rails at z=20" directly above the tank (same x/y footprint, vertically stacked). The forward 10" of PC is a dedicated service bay containing the pump/accumulator/filter cluster: the 12V pump sits on the floor, the accumulator stacks vertically above it (same x/y footprint, different z), and the inline filter sits beside the accumulator at the same elevation. This stacking keeps the cluster fingertip-accessible from a single forward-facing service door without sacrificing tank or heater capacity.

The piping goal is the straightest possible runs. The cold manifold tee sits at the forward edge of the service bay just past the filter, producing three direct outputs: (1) C-4a loops up to the top of PC and rearward along the ceiling back to the WH cold input (BSPP) at the rear of the heater; (2) C-4b runs straight forward through the PC/G bulkhead to the under-sink cold stub-up; (3) C-4c drops to the floor and runs straight rearward along the inside passenger wall to the outdoor shower mixer on the rear face of PC. The hot tee at the front of the WH does the same for hot: H-1a straight forward to the sink hot stub; H-1b up and rearward along the inside driver wall to the shower mixer hot side. The low-point drain ball valve taps at the lowest point of the cluster (floor level beside the pump) and drops straight through a sealed grommet to the underbody — no horizontal floor run required.

Critical install notes

PEX-A memory shrink: after expansion, let the connection rest 2-3 minutes at room temperature before pressurizing. The pipe shrinks back over the brass teeth and forms a permanent seal. Do not rush this step.

Anchor near the pump: cushioned P-clips within 6-12 inches of the pump inlet and outlet kill vibration transfer down the line. Without this the rigid pipe broadcasts pump noise through the entire passenger wall.

Vent the gray tank: the 5-gallon portable inside G-A must have a small cracked top vent. A fully sealed tank glug-drains and the sink will run slow.

Low-point drain accessibility: the ball valve handle must be reachable without removing cabinet panels. Mount it just inside the PC floor access, oriented so the handle clears any plumbing or wiring above it.

No CCA or crimp on PEX-A: only expansion fittings. Crimp rings and cinch clamps are spec'd for PEX-B, not PEX-A; mixing systems will leak.

Cushion-clip the BSPP adapters at the WH: Isotemp casings use a metric thread profile that PEX-A brass FNPT adapters will not seal against. Use dedicated BSPP-to-PEX-A adapters and seal with PTFE tape rated for hot water.

Test pressure before closing cabinets: charge the system to operating pressure (40-50 psi) and walk every joint with a paper towel. Drips show immediately on white paper. Fix any seep before paneling goes back on.
