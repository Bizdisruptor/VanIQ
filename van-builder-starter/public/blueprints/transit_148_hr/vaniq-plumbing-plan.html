Pressure core layout (PC)

The water system is intentionally clustered in PC to minimize total run length, simplify service access, and isolate noise. The rear face of PC carries all external interfaces (city water inlet on top, outdoor shower mixer below). The forward face of the pressure cluster (pump → accumulator → filter) sits in a tight line on a single service bracket, with the Isotemp Slim 25L water heater on rails directly above it. Cold and hot manifold tees sit at the forward edge of the pressure cluster so all four destination lines (WH cold, sink cold, shower cold from the cold tee; sink hot, shower hot from the hot tee) start from a single accessible point. The low-point drain ball valve taps the cold manifold and runs straight to a sealed floor grommet for gravity winterization.

Critical install notes

PEX-A memory shrink: after expansion, let the connection rest 2-3 minutes at room temperature before pressurizing. The pipe shrinks back over the brass teeth and forms a permanent seal. Do not rush this step.

Anchor near the pump: cushioned P-clips within 6-12 inches of the pump inlet and outlet kill vibration transfer down the line. Without this the rigid pipe broadcasts pump noise through the entire passenger wall.

Vent the gray tank: the 5-gallon portable inside G-A must have a small cracked top vent. A fully sealed tank glug-drains and the sink will run slow.

Low-point drain accessibility: the ball valve handle must be reachable without removing cabinet panels. Mount it just inside the PC floor access, oriented so the handle clears any plumbing or wiring above it.

No CCA or crimp on PEX-A: only expansion fittings. Crimp rings and cinch clamps are spec'd for PEX-B, not PEX-A; mixing systems will leak.

Cushion-clip the BSPP adapters at the WH: Isotemp casings use a metric thread profile that PEX-A brass FNPT adapters will not seal against. Use dedicated BSPP-to-PEX-A adapters and seal with PTFE tape rated for hot water.

Test pressure before closing cabinets: charge the system to operating pressure (40-50 psi) and walk every joint with a paper towel. Drips show immediately on white paper. Fix any seep before paneling goes back on.
